The 12D Sales Playbook

Mastering the discovery, diagnostic, and close for The Twelve Dimensional Path — a system of operation, not a script to memorize.

Version 1.1 Updated May 2026 Sections 16
I.

The Mission

We aren't selling a course. We're providing a System of Operation.

Most prospects are running on a legacy 3D Autopilot program that is inefficient and draining. Your job is to audit their current mental state, identify the Leaks, and bridge them to the 12D solution.

You are not their friend. You are not their guru. You are a consultant on their state of consciousness. That posture is what earns the close.

III.

Pre-Call Preparation

Spend 5 minutes before every call. Walking in cold is how deals die.

Review Before Dialing

  • Their Skool engagement — which posts have they commented on, what content have they consumed?
  • The free training or video that brought them in — assume that's their entry point and reference it directly.
  • Any DM or application answers they submitted.
  • Their public profile if available — location, occupation, life stage.

Mental Prep

  • Sit silent for 60 seconds before the call. Drop into the Observational Mind yourself. If you are in 3D Autopilot, you cannot move them out of it.
  • Review the prospect's stated reason for joining. Anchor the opener to it.

Set the Environment

  • No notifications. No second screen with email. Phone face down.
  • Have the script and objection map open in one tab. Pricing and offer details in another.
IV.

Lead Qualification

Not every lead deserves a 45-minute call. Filter before you invest.

Qualified Prospect
  • Has consumed at least one piece of Elizabeth's content
  • Has articulated a specific friction — focus, exhaustion, meaning, stuckness
  • Has agency over their own finances and decisions
  • Engaged with the booking flow themselves, not pushed by someone else
Disqualified Before the Call
  • Booked on a whim with no content engagement
  • Currently in acute mental health crisis (see Red Flags)
  • Looking for medical or therapeutic intervention
  • Cannot make a decision without a third party who isn't on the call
V.

The Sales Path

The architecture of the call. Four steps, roughly 30–45 minutes total.

01
The Frame
~ 2 min
Set the tone. Don't be a friend — be a consultant.
Opener Hey [Name], Travis here. I saw you engaged with the 30-Second Shift video. My goal today is simple — I want to see if that shift actually clicked for you, or if your current 3D program is still running the show. Ready to dive in?
02
The Audit
15–20 min
Discovery and diagnostics. You are not here to inform — you are here to diagnose.
Noise Check Scale of 1 to 10 — how loud is your internal chatter right now? Does it feel like a Brain Radio you can't turn off?
Leak Identification When you're working, how often does your focus snap? Every time that happens, you're leaking energy. Does that explain why you're exhausted by 3:00 PM?
Objective

Get them to admit, in their own words, that the current system is failing. Until they say it, you can't sell the fix.

03
The Bridge
5–10 min
Frame 12D as a performance upgrade, not a belief system.
The Logic The reason you're stuck isn't lack of effort — it's high-overhead energy usage. Your Narrative Mind is burning 90% of your fuel. We teach you how to switch to the Observational Mind, which is a zero-friction state. It's like upgrading your brain's operating system.
04
The Commitment
5–10 min
Match the offer to the leak severity. Default to VIP unless they're genuinely testing the waters.
VIP / The Lab — primary close Based on what you've told me, VIP is where you belong. It's $999 for the year — which is actually less than paying Premium monthly — and I'm including a 1:1 call with Elizabeth herself. This is the exact toolset you need to stop the leaks and regain your focus. Do we get you started today?
Premium / The Classroom — fallback close Let's start you in Premium at $97/mo so you can experience the work without a year-long commitment. You can always step up to VIP later.
VI.

Discovery Question Bank

He who asks the questions, controls the state. Use these to deepen the audit.

Surface · Warm-up
  • What pulled you into Elizabeth's world in the first place?
  • What's the moment you realized something needed to change?
Diagnostic · Find the Leak
  • When was the last time you felt fully present — not chasing the next thing?
  • Walk me through the last 24 hours. Where did your attention go that you wish it hadn't?
  • What's the cost of staying exactly where you are for another year?
Stakes · Amplify
  • If nothing changes, what does your life look like in five years?
  • Who else pays the price when you're operating at half-capacity?
Vision · Paint the Contrast
  • If the noise stopped tomorrow, what would you actually do with that bandwidth?
  • What does your life look like when you're the author and not the character?
Commitment · Test Readiness
  • Scale of 1–10, how committed are you to actually fixing this — not just talking about it?
  • If I told you the path required 15 minutes a day, every day, are you in?

VIII.

Handling Objections

Click any objection to reveal the reframe. Search by keyword in the sidebar.

8.1
"It's too expensive."
+
I hear you. But let's look at the math first. Premium runs $97/mo — that's $1,164 a year. VIP is $999 flat and includes full library access worth $15,000, Advanced Remote Viewing, and quarterly session discounts. So the "expensive" option is actually the cheaper one. The real question is — how much mental capital are you wasting every day right now? If we plug those leaks and you become 20% more effective, this pays for itself in weeks. This isn't a cost — it's an investment in your primary asset: your mind.
8.2
"I don't have the time."
+
That is exactly the point. You don't have time because your 3D Autopilot is wasting it. You're operating at half-speed. This protocol gives you your time back by making your awareness razor-sharp. Can you afford not to fix this?
8.3
"Is this just more meditation?"
+
Not even close. Meditation is passive. This is active mental engineering. We aren't sitting still — we're learning to navigate different dimensions of awareness to produce real-world results. It's the difference between looking at a car and learning how to drive it.
8.4
"Let me think about it."
+
Of course — but let me ask: what specifically do you need to think about? Is it the money, the time, or whether the protocol actually works for someone like you? Let's resolve the real question right now while I'm on the line.
8.5
"I need to talk to my spouse / partner."
+
Totally fair. Before we get off, what do you think they'll ask? Let's get you the answers now so you're not playing telephone. And — is your partner the decision-maker here, or are they a sounding board?
8.6
"I've tried programs like this before and they didn't work."
+
That actually tells me you're closer than most. You've already proven you're willing to invest in yourself. The reason the others didn't work is they were 3D solutions to a 12D problem. What did the previous program ask you to do? … Right — and that's exactly why it failed.
8.7
"I'm not really sure I believe in the spiritual stuff."
+
Good. We don't ask you to believe anything. The Observational Mind isn't a belief — it's a state you can verify in 30 seconds. If the protocol works, it works whether you call it spiritual or neurological. The labels are 3D — the results aren't.
8.8
"Can I just stay in the free group for now?"
+
Absolutely, and many people do. But notice what just happened — your Narrative Mind just talked you out of the upgrade. That's the exact leak we're trying to plug. The free group teaches you what's possible. The paid container is where the shift actually happens.
8.9
"Send me some information and I'll review it."
+
I'd rather not — and here's why. Information isn't your bottleneck. You already have enough information to know something needs to change, or you wouldn't be on this call. What you need is a decision. Let's make it here.
IX.

Red Flags & Disqualification

Your time is valuable — and so is the integrity of the community. Walk away when you should.

High-Resistance Profile Low-Fit · Time Sink

  • Constant victim mentality with no ownership
  • Refuses to try the 30-second exercises
  • Argues over mechanics without engaging
  • Has been "shopping" multiple spiritual programs without committing to any
Exit Script · High-Resistance It sounds like you're comfortable with your current Autopilot program for now. No problem. Stay in the free group, keep watching the content, and when you're ready to take the wheel, reach back out. Best of luck.

Hard-Stop Profile Do Not Enroll

Non-Negotiable

These are absolute. Enrolling these prospects creates harm and exposes the business — even if they have the money and want to pay. Their consent is not the same as their readiness.

  • Active mental health crisis — suicidal ideation, severe depression, psychosis, recent psychiatric hospitalization
  • Looking for therapy or medical treatment — they need a licensed professional, not a community
  • Currently in withdrawal or active substance abuse
  • Acute grief or trauma within the last 90 days without professional support
  • Cognitive impairment that compromises informed consent
  • Pressured by a third party to join against their own judgment
Exit Script · Hard-Stop What you're describing sounds like something that needs more direct, professional support than this community is designed to provide. The 12D Path works best alongside that foundation, not as a replacement for it. I'd encourage you to start there, and the door here stays open.

Document any hard-stop on the lead record and flag for Elizabeth's review.

XII.

Post-Close Handoff

The close is the start of the relationship, not the end.

Within 5 Minutes of Close

  • Process payment while on the line
  • Send confirmation email with login + first-step instructions
  • Add them to the appropriate Skool group / cohort

Within 24 Hours

  • Welcome message from Elizabeth or the community team
  • Calendar invite to next live call
  • Onboarding checklist sent

Within 7 Days

  • Personal check-in — "How did the first ritual land?"
  • Flag any early disengagement to the success team
Why This Matters

A clean handoff prevents buyer's remorse and refund requests. Most refunds are not about the product — they're about feeling abandoned after the sale.

XIII.

Tonality & Delivery

The script is 30% of the call. How you deliver it is the other 70%.

  • Pace — Slower than feels natural. Silence is your tool. After a diagnostic question, do not fill the pause. Let them sit in it.
  • Pitch — Lower register signals authority. Especially on the close.
  • Energy — Calm, certain, grounded. You are the lighthouse — they are the boat. The lighthouse doesn't chase.
  • Mirroring — Match their pace for the first 5 minutes, then lead them down. If they're frantic, you get slower.
  • Smile — Audibly. It changes the resonance of your voice.
Three Deadly Sins
  1. Over-talking — every word past the point of close is a word that can lose the sale.
  2. Pitch-rising at the end of statements. Makes you sound uncertain.
  3. Apologizing for the price.
XIV.

Ethics & Compliance

Non-negotiable. Violations are grounds for removal from the team.

Never Claim
  • Medical or therapeutic outcomes — "this will cure your anxiety," "this fixes depression"
  • Specific income, performance, or relationship results
  • That the program substitutes for professional mental health care, medical care, or financial advice
  • Anything Elizabeth has not personally claimed in her own teaching
Always
  • Be honest about what the program does and doesn't do
  • Respect a "no" the first time — second-time pressure is coercion, not persuasion
  • Document the call accurately in the CRM
  • Disclose payment terms clearly before processing
  • Honor the refund policy without friction

Vulnerable prospects get redirected, never enrolled. Their consent is not the same as their readiness. Long-term reputation outranks short-term commission. Every aggressive close that should have been a "not yet" costs us a refund, a bad review, and harms someone who trusted us.
